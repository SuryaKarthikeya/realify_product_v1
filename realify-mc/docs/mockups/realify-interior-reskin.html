The five lenses
Principal-designer note. The interior currently runs a cool-gray / navy / Inter system with heavy drop-shadows — a different visual world from the marketing site's warm paper / charcoal / terracotta / Georgia-serif language (extracted live: ink #1A1A1A, paper #F7F4EE, terracotta #C4785B, 9px radius, sand pills). This reskin brings the interior into that one system so a prospect who loved the site doesn't feel like they logged into a different product. Content, data, and layout are unchanged — only the skin. Semantic colors (scale/fix/cut, health bands) are kept but re-tuned to the warm family. Click through the five lenses.
